about

Designer, maker and creative technologist.

I am currently pursuing a Master of Design Studies (Mediums) at the Harvard Graduate School of Design, and currently a visiting student at the MIT Media Lab Tangible Media Group focusing on the intersection of design and technology, particularly Human–Computer Interaction, User Experience Design and AI.

I have an interdisciplinary background in HCI, AI product design, and XR/AR/VR interaction development, with full-cycle project experience covering AI hardware interaction design, UX research, full-stack development, and data visualization. I have worked on AI product development, intelligent interaction interfaces, and XR system design at world-class institutions including the MIT Media Lab, Harvard GSD, and NYU Future Reality Lab, as well as at HDR. My work has been published in top conferences such as NeurIPS, CVPR, CHI, UIST, DIS, TEI, and ISMAR.

I have received multiple international design and creativity awards and am well-qualified for roles such as Interaction Designer and AI Product Manager.

Combining software development skills with design thinking, I bridge the gap between development and design to bring ideas from concept to product realization. I value independent thinking and clear communication, while maintaining a humble attitude toward continuous learning and collaboration.
