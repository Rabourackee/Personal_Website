Color Phonograph
Time2020

Program
NYUShanghai IMA Interactions Lab
Team
Krabs Xiao
This is an installation in the appearance of a phonograph. It reads the input record drawn onsite by the audience and transfers the colors to music. By manipulating the relationship between visual forms and audio forms, this installation provides a novel definition of understanding both color and music.
