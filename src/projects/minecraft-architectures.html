Personal Project
This castle is one of the main projects in my architectural playing experiences in Minecraft. Its style is a mixture of Gothic, Renaissance, Chinese, and Indian, and took inspiration from Hayao Miyazaki's animations. Every block inside is delicately designed, taking 2 years to complete the building process.
