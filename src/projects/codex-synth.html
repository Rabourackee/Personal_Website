CODEX-2AI MODEL
BUILDER

human–ai co-creation in cad. model with the tools you know, and let ai generate, edit and iterate on the model with you.

CODEX explores the interaction patterns of future AI CAD tools, merging AI 3D generation into the CAD modeling workflow. It was the first research and prototype for AI software products at the D3 group.

open codex-2 ↗

02

cad is precise. and slow.

a study of the tools designers use every day.

  1. precise

    The model format defines every boundary exactly.

  2. detailed

    The process gives control over every dimension.

  3. manual

    Every step needs a hand on it, from first line to last.

the pain point

the hours go into the hundred ordinary models around the one that matters.

Much of the time is spent building standard models that don’t need the most care, and aren’t the core of the design.

the idea

ai-powered cad.

AI can produce the standard models without anyone touching every detail, so human effort goes into the designs that need it.

03

a mesh, or a model?

ai can already make 3d. what it hands back is the problem. the same house, both ways:

drag either one to turn both.

so codex-2 joins two workflows

  1. ai mesh generationfast, from a prompt
  2. cad modelingexact, editable
  3. ai cad generationthe speed of one, the format and quality of the other

04

three functions, three promises

from what users need to what the interface must be.

pain point

CAD tools lack automation and agency.

main goal

Use AI to enhance the modeling workflow, and free people from repetitive, standard routines.

critical functions

design goals

01

basic cad

The essential CAD functions, so a model can be edited the usual way.

familiar

Keep CAD’s interface elements, so CAD users can learn it and try it at once.

02

ai agent

The core: write the commands and build the model from a prompt.

intuitive

Make talking to the AI easy to understand and quick to use.

03

merging system

One system for what the AI makes and what you model by hand.

robust

A registry and document system that link AI models and CAD models without seams.

05

the interface

cad you already know, with an agent at the centre. point at a part.

06

a working model of the interface, built around the magic pen: draw around something, then say what you want. start with the three tasks the pen was designed for.
