Before You Begin
Time2022

Program
NYU ITP
Team
Xuanyu Hao, Sinuo Chen
This VR gaming experience focuses on the relationship between the player and the avatar. We take it for granted that the avatar is controlled by the player. What if the avatar has its own feelings and thoughts, what made the avatar be controlled by the player, and how did the perspective transition between the player as god and the avatar as creatures happen? These are the questions that we want to ask the players that come to experience this game. The game is developed in Unreal Engine and played on an Oculus II VR headset.
