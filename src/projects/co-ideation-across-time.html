Co-Ideation Across Time
Time2025-2026

Program
MIT Media Lab Tangible Media Group

Team:
Quincy Kuang, Lucy Li, Paul P


Co-Ideation Across Time is a Human–AI interface for brainstorming and creative critique. It allows users to collaborate with an AI mentor modeled on the ideas and personality of Professor Hiroshi Ishii.

The system is built from thousands of his manuscripts, lectures, papers, and public talks. Users can sketch, write, or describe their ideas, and the AI responds with feedback grounded in his design philosophy—offering critique, questions, and suggestions for further development.

The goal is not to replace mentorship, but to extend it—making dialogue with a mentor possible across time and distance.

This project is ongoing, with current work focused on making the interaction more intuitive and engaging.
