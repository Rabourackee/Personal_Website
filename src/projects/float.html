03

from a click to a box in the air

every virtual box on the screen has one real box in the room, and one motor between them.

  1. the screen

    A block world on a computer. Put a box where you want it, as in a game.

  2. arduino

    The computer sends the new heights; the Arduino turns them into steps.

  3. 16 stepper motors

    One per box, each turning exactly as far as it is told.

  4. a box in the air

    The box rises or falls on its lines, to the place it was given on the screen.

04

like minecraft, in a room

When we move things in a game or a modelling tool, nothing in the room moves. What if a physical model answered to the virtual one, at the scale of architecture?

FLOAT connects a virtual space directly to a physical one. As in Minecraft, you put blocks where you like; here each block is also a real box, in front of you, in real time. The boxes form one structure that seems to float: an interactive architecture, shaped by whoever is at the screen.

It gives a virtual environment a body: tangible interaction, something to touch and walk around, and an output you see, feel and stand inside, not only look at.

  1. tangiblethe virtual move becomes a physical one
  2. hapticthe space you build is there to touch
  3. multi-modalsight, touch and the space around you, at once

Architecture not merely inhabited, but co-created: a partner in creation, responsive to and shaped by the people inside it.
