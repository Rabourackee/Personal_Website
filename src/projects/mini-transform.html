While the original TRANSFORM occupies the scale of a large desk, Mini TRANSFORM reimagines the system as a compact, hand-held device. It is designed to be graspable and playable, bringing shape-changing interaction into a more personal and tactile scale.

We re-engineered the mechanical structure of each pixel and minimized the overall architecture, to reach the smallest possible form factor while keeping movement and responsiveness.
