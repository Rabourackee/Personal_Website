AR Monuments
Time2023

Affiliation
NYUShanghai Digital Heritage Lab


This AR project presents the audience with the immersive experience of traditional Chinese architecture: the first section features interactive AR annotations based on the physical model of the Dougong structures, the second section introduces classical Chinese architectural examples into the exhibition space, the audience can see the structure of these architectures from a perspective not easily seen by the tourists. This header photo shows the perspective of the Guanyin statue from inside the Guanyin pavilion.
