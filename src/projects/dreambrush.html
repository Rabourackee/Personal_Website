01序prelude

what happens when a brushstroke is a word, and the word becomes the gesture that brings a picture into being?

Dream Brush is a generative painting experience. You write Chinese characters with a real calligraphy brush, dipped only in water, on a canvas mounted on an easel. The canvas shows the trace of your stroke, then lets it fade as if drying. In its place, the system paints: each character becomes a motif of Chinese painting, with sound.

Generative tools are pushing creative work toward detached prompting. Chinese characters offer another way in: a single mark can carry meaning and still be a gesture and a picture. What you write, and where, how large and at what angle you write it, all steer what appears.

Writing and painting are different names for the same body.

after Zhang Yanyuan, Records of Famous Paintings of All Dynasties, c. 847

梦笔生花

The name echoes an old idiom. As a boy, the poet Li Bai dreamt that flowers bloomed from the tip of his brush.

02写write

choose a character. brush where it goes.

A working model of the canvas. Pick a character, then drag across the paper: the size and place of your stroke are the size and place of the change. Write a second character into the scene and the painting grows around it.

the canvas suggests

a model, not the installation: motifs are drawn in your browser, and nothing is recognised from handwriting. The real system reads the written character with a vision model and paints it with an image model in about ten seconds.

03法four rules

one inscription, four meanings.

Each character is read through coupled constraints. A single gesture carries what a screen would split into tool choice, prompt wording and parameters.

  1. sizehow much of the painting is repainted.
  2. locationwhere the change happens.
  3. contextreplace, modify or add, depending on what is already there.
  4. semanticsa literal picture, or a shift in weather or time of day.

06理system

from a stroke to a scene.

  1. 01captureThe handwritten character is captured as an image.
  2. 02readA vision model recognises it. The text becomes the concept prompt.
  3. 03apaintThe stroke's place is marked with a bounding region. The image model paints the concept inside it and keeps the rest of the picture stable.
    03bsoundIn parallel, a text-to-sound model composes a sound effect or a short melody.
  4. 04revealWhen both are ready, the projection changes and the sound plays.

07时time

the waiting is the drying.

Painting a new image takes about ten seconds, long enough to collapse into waiting. So the live stroke appears at once, and fades like water drying while the painting is made. The delay becomes part of the work: a rhythm of mark, fade and response, closer to following a material than executing a plan.

08思reflections

what people did with it.

  1. easier than a prompt.

    People write straight into the area they want, instead of describing a place in words. Many characters are near-square, so turning, scaling or combining them reshapes the target area.

  2. a shared game.

    Two groups of high school students agreed together on what to paint next. When an update landed, they cheered; when it missed, the gap between intention, recognition and painting became a joke and a puzzle to solve together.

    airplanetrainnothingcancellation

    words people tried, to test the edges: anachronisms and negations.

  3. where it goes next.

    Shrink the rectangle to the written shape, so irregular areas can be targeted. Let changes bleed in like ink, not cut. Write short prose or poetry for more abstract answers. Carry the idea to other writing systems.
