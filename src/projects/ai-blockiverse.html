This is an immersive interactive experience design that combines artificial intelligence with environmental education, aiming to engage children and families in exploring the importance of ocean conservation and sustainability through play. In a shared imme﻿rsive space, children and parents can move physical blocks together, generating virtual ocean assets such as coral, fish, and debris in real-time, visually demonstrating how small actions can impact ecosystems.

Guided by the concept of "from simplicity to magnificence," the experience encourages parent-child collaboration and community interaction, allowing participants to observe ecological changes: debris causing coral to fade, and fish multiplying near healthy coral. Artificial intelligence generates personalized stories based on each interaction, helping family members intuitively understand the environmental impact of their actions and reflect on the importance of ecological protection.

Blockiverse combines hands-on activities, dynamic storytelling, and community engagement to foster empathy and environmental responsibility, offering children and communities an engaging and meaningful educational journey.
