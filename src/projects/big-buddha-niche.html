Big Buddha Niche
Time2022-2023

Affiliation
NYUShanghai Digital Heritage Lab

Team
Personal Project

This project reimagines an existed but long-vanished cave eave structure outside of the Big Buddha Niche at Huangze Temple, Sichuan, China. The existence of the structure was proved by the traces on the cliff. After research, I inferred from the remaining drill holes that the existed structure was built in the early Tang Dynasty. With the help of the Dunhuang Grottos and the relevant materials of ancient Chinese architecture, I was able to rebuild the possible model of the existed structure in detail.
